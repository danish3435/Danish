Ingredients to make Pecan Cake

  • Cake flour
  • Brown sugar
  • Buttermilk
  • Cream cheese
  • Pecans

    Steps of making Pecan Cake 101

    • 1. Melt butter over medium-low heat.
    • 2. Increase the heat and cook, stirring frequently, as the butter begins to foam and sizzle.
      3. Once you see brown specks forming on the bottom of the pan, remove the butter from the heat and pour into a heatproof bowl to cool completely
    • 4. Measure your pecans and place them in the bowl of a food processor.
    • 5. Pulse until the pecans are fine crumbs, then stop. Set aside 1 ½ cups for the cake and ⅓ cup for the decoration.
    • 6. Whisk together the dry ingredients in a large bowl.
    • 7. Add the browned butter and stir with the mixer on low speed until incorporated.
    • 8. Combine the remaining wet ingredients in a separate bowl, then gradually add them to the dry ingredients, pausing halfway through to scrape the bowl.
    • 9. Add the pecans and stir until incorporated.
